## Root cause and completed repair

The generated island was not failing to generate or stream the next technical region. The production seam was proved directly before changing the renderer: seed 20001 could move the player across a 128x128 streaming-region boundary, `WorldStreamingCoordinator` followed the new focus, authoritative terrain existed at the target, and the bounded renderer eventually shifted correctly.

The actual live defect was `LargeAreaRenderWindowController` using a fixed **12-cell** recenter buffer around an **80x96-cell** render window. That policy watched only the camera center. On a tall phone viewport, and increasingly when zoomed out, the visible screen could reach beyond the bounded render window before the camera center came within 12 cells of its edge. The result looked exactly like “new cells are not rendering” even though world materialization and streaming were healthy.

Current production contract:

- `LargeAreaRenderWindowController` computes the recenter margin from actual viewport pixel size, current camera zoom, cell-pixel size and render-window dimensions;
- the historical 12-cell buffer remains a minimum safety floor;
- a two-cell presentation safety allowance is added beyond the calculated half-viewport footprint;
- the margin is clamped below half the render-window dimension so a valid centered window remains possible;
- recentering therefore happens before the visible camera footprint can expose the unrendered bounded-window edge;
- zooming out can increase, never decrease, the required coverage margin;
- no world-generation owner, streaming-region geometry, materialization identity, perception truth, movement, collision, lighting, utility or simulation-time system was replaced.

For the reported phone-class portrait viewport **832x1792** at normal zoom and the production 24-pixel cells / 80x96 render window, the deterministic margin is **20 cells horizontally and 40 vertically**, rather than the old fixed 12/12.
